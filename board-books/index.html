For new parents · 100 picks

Board books worth chewing

Books a baby can do something with: touch-and-feel pages, lift-the-flaps, felt flaps, peekaboo holes, pull tabs, finger puppets and mirrors. Ranked.

How it's rankedNo authoritative top 100 exists, and most online lists are affiliate blogs. The classics those lists keep repeating come first: Pat the Bunny, Dear Zoo, Spot, Peek-a-Who?, "That's Not My…", Karen Katz, Van Fleet, DK.
Series are swappableWhere a series has dozens of titles, the one listed is usually the one libraries stock. Any sister title works the same way.
Borrowing in ConnecticutThe links open the Library Connection catalog: about 30 Hartford-area libraries with free holds between them. Libraries buy few flap books because they get torn, so expect some wear.
