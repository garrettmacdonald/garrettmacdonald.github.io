What I do

Four disciplines

01SAA

Strategic asset allocation

Long-horizon policy portfolios, built with constrained optimization across return, risk and the real-world limits that actually bind.

02TAA

Tactical asset allocation

Sized, deliberate tilts around the policy portfolio, taken only when the evidence is strong enough to pay for the risk.

03Passive

Passive equity

Index portfolios run for precision: tight tracking, low cost, and clean, repeatable implementation.

04Active

Active equity

Equity portfolios where every active position is intentional, sized to a risk budget, and measured against its benchmark.
