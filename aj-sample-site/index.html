Planning ahead

Heat pump, EV or induction range on the list? Start with the panel.

A lot of homes around here were wired for 100 amps and oil or gas heat. Electrifying adds big loads, and the panel is where they all meet. Before you buy, we'll look at what you have and what you're adding, run the load calculation, and lay out the options in writing.

  • Upgrade to 200 A with spare spaces planned for what comes next
  • Load management for an EV charger when a full upgrade isn't needed

Common questions
How much does a panel upgrade cost?

It depends on the house: the amperage, overhead or underground service, where the meter is, and how many circuits move. In 2026, most 200 A upgrades we do run $X,XXX–$X,XXX, permit and inspection included. Text us photos of your panel and meter and we can usually give you a firm price the same day.

Do I need 200 amps for a heat pump?

Often, but not always. It depends on what's already in the house, the size of the heat pump, and whether an EV charger or induction range is coming too. We'll run the load calculation and show you the numbers.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We pull the electrical permit with your town on every job that needs one, coordinate with Eversource on service work, and meet the inspector.

Do you take emergency calls?

Yes. For no power, a buzzing or hot panel, or outlets that smell like burning, call 860-690-9472 any time. If you see smoke or flames, get out and call 911 first.

Are you licensed and insured?
