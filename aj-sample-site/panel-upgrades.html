Signs it’s time

When a panel upgrade makes sense.

You’re adding a big loadHeat pump, EV charger, induction range, hot tub or an addition
No open spacesDoubled-up breakers, or a subpanel squeezed in somewhere
A problem-brand panelFederal Pacific, Zinsco and other panels with known breaker issues
Fuses or a small service60–100 A on a house that’s grown since it was wired
Something’s offBreakers that trip often, a warm cover, lights that dim or flicker
Someone flagged itYour insurer, a home inspection, or a buyer’s inspector
What’s included

Everything to get it done and signed off.

  • New panel with more spaces than you need today
  • New meter socket and service entrance where needed
  • Whole-home surge protector (now required by code on service work)
  • Grounding and bonding brought up to current code
  • Town permit, Eversource disconnect and reconnect, inspection
  • Every circuit traced and labeled on a typed directory
  • Clean work area and photos of the finished panel for your records
  • 2-year workmanship warranty, in writing

Do you really need 200 A?

We’ll show you the math.

Not every house needs a bigger service. Sometimes a larger panel on the same service is enough, or load management for one big appliance. We’ll run a load calculation on what you have and what you’re adding, and lay out the options in writing before you spend anything.

Homes with solar

Tell us about your solar when you ask for a quote. We reconnect it to the new panel to code, put the required labels back on, and make sure your monitoring still reads correctly.

How it goes

Photos to inspection, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Photos & quote

    Send photos of the panel (door open), the meter, and where the wires come into the house. Most quotes go out the same day.

  2. 02

    Scheduling

    We book your install day, pull the permit and line up Eversource. Typical lead time is 1–2 weeks.

  3. 03

    Install day

    Power is off for part of the day, usually 6–8 hours. We’ll tell you when, so you can plan around the fridge and Wi-Fi.

  4. 04

    Inspection

    We meet the town inspector and close out the permit. You get the labeled directory and photos of the work.

Questions

Panel upgrade FAQ

How much does a 200 A upgrade cost?

In 2026, most of ours run $X,XXX–$X,XXX, with the permit, Eversource coordination, surge protector and inspection included. Price depends on overhead vs. underground service, the meter location, panel size, and how many circuits move. Photos usually get you a firm number the same day.

Do I need to call Eversource?

No. We submit the paperwork and schedule the disconnect and reconnect.

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let us in at the start and we need clear access to the panel and the meter. After that, many customers head to work.

Will my generator, solar or EV charger still work?

Yes. Tell us what you have and we’ll plan for it in the new panel, with the right breaker and labels for each.

Can I finance it?

Often, yes, when the upgrade is part of a heat pump or energy project: Connecticut Green Bank’s Smart-E loans run through participating contractors. Ask us about financing when you get your quote.

Can I get a bigger panel without a full service upgrade?

Sometimes. If the load calculation says your service can carry what you’re adding, a panel replacement with more spaces can be the cheaper answer.
