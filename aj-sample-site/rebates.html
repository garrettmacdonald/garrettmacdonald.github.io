Rebates & incentives

What’s actually available in Connecticut in 2026.

Programs changed a lot this year, and plenty of websites are out of date. Here’s the short version, with links to the official pages. When we quote, we’ll tell you what applies to your job.

Last reviewed: October 2026

Heat pumps

Energize CT Residential Energy Optimization pays a per-ton rebate, up to $10,000 per home, when a cold-climate heat pump replaces oil, propane, gas or electric baseboard heat. It must be installed by a contractor in Energize CT’s Heat Pump Installer Network, so it runs through your HVAC company.

A smaller standard rebate covers heat pump swaps and cooling-only systems.

Energize CT heat pump rebates

EV chargers

Eversource managed charging pays you for charging off-peak. Many smart chargers and newer EVs can enroll, and we can set your charger up for it.

Eversource’s charger and wiring rebates have been limited to income-eligible customers and designated areas since January 1, 2026.

Eversource CT EV programs

Panel & service upgrades

There’s no stand-alone Connecticut rebate for a panel upgrade right now. When the upgrade is part of a heat pump project, it can often be financed with the project.

CT Green Bank Smart-E loans

Home batteries

Energy Storage Solutions pays upfront and performance incentives for home batteries, a quieter alternative or companion to a generator. Program terms changed April 1, 2026.

Energy Storage Solutions

Federal tax credits have ended. The 25C credit for heat pumps and related panel work ended December 31, 2025. The 30C credit for home EV chargers ended for equipment placed in service after June 30, 2026. If a website still promises “30% back from the IRS,” it’s out of date.

Program rules and amounts change. Check the official pages above before you buy; we’re electricians, not tax advisors.
