Service area

Every town’s houses need something different.

We’re based in West Hartford and work across the Farmington Valley and Greater Hartford, within about 15 miles. Here’s what we tend to see in each town, and why.

Get a free quote 860-690-9472

West Hartford

Home base

Half of West Hartford’s homes were built by 1954, and nearly four in five before 1970. Many still have the 60–100 A service they were built with, now asked to carry central air, an EV and sometimes a heat pump. The town also has the sixth-most electric vehicles in Connecticut.

  • 200 A service upgrades
  • Kitchen & bath wiring
  • Recessed lighting
  • EV chargers
  • Heat pumps replacing gas

Farmington & Unionville

Next door

Newer homes than West Hartford (median built 1980), but nearly half still heat with oil or propane: prime candidates for a heat pump, which usually means a look at the panel first. About one vehicle in twenty is electric.

  • Heat pump circuits & panels
  • EV chargers
  • Basement & remodel wiring
  • Small commercial

Avon

Farmington Valley

Avon has the highest share of electric vehicles in the area, and almost half its homes heat with oil or propane. It also knows outages: Tropical Storm Isaias in 2020 knocked out power to nearly the whole town.

  • EV chargers
  • Standby generators & batteries
  • Heat pump wiring
  • 200 A upgrades

Simsbury & Weatogue

Farmington Valley

More than half of Simsbury’s homes heat with oil or propane, and it leads nearby towns in heat pumps installed per household. Plenty of 1950s–60s houses too, so the panel often needs work before the heat pump goes in.

  • Heat pump circuits & panels
  • Generators
  • Service upgrades

Wethersfield & Newington

South of West Hartford

Some of the oldest housing around: 70% of Wethersfield’s homes and over half of Newington’s were built before 1970. Older wiring and small services are the common thread, and EV ownership is growing fast in both.

  • Service upgrades
  • Rewiring older homes
  • Problem-brand panels
  • EV chargers

Canton, Burlington & Granby

Upper valley

Mostly oil and propane heat (more than 80% of homes in Burlington and Granby) and long rural lines that go down in storms. Backup power and heat pump conversions are the big projects here.

  • Standby generators
  • Heat pump wiring
  • Generator inlets
  • Outbuildings & barns

Also serving

Bloomfield, Hartford, New Britain, Windsor and other nearby towns. Not sure if you’re in range? Text us your town.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 (year built, heating fuel); CT DEEP electric vehicle registrations, December 2025; Energize CT heat pump data; PURA Docket 20-08-03 (Isaias outages).
