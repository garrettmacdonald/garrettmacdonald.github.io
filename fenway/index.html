    A little about the guy

    He's from Strafford, Vermont.A town of about a thousand people. Most of what he writes is about New England: small towns, long winters, leaving and coming back.
    Fenway is his home field.He first played it in 2024 and put those nights out as a live album. This run started as four shows, and he added two more the day we bought.
    “The Last of the Bugs”is the expanded edition of his 2026 album The Great Divide, and the name of this tour: 31 stadium dates, starting April 24 in Athens, Georgia.
    He started The Busyhead Project,which raises money for mental health care. He talks about it from the stage most nights.
