Three things to decide before noon

The cart gives you about 4 to 5 minutes. That's not the time to talk it over.

1 · How many

Suggested: 2. Go to 4 only if the other couple has said a firm yes. Tickets can't be transferred, and whoever buys has to walk in with the whole group. All the tickets go in one order.

Suggested: yes. Then one phone waits for Sunday from the start, as our safety net. Our rule if Sunday gets in first: put seats in the cart, give Saturday 3 more minutes, and if Saturday still isn't in, buy Sunday and be happy.

Tuesday, minute by minute

  2. 11:40On each device: open the Ticketmaster selections page, tap the copy icon next to that device's code, then tap the › arrow for the date. It opens the Red Sox Noah Kahan page.
  3. 11:45In position on all four. Look under "Artist Presale" for the button to buy. If it's not there yet, refresh every minute until it is. Enter the code as soon as a box appears.
  4. 12:00Go. Expect a waiting page with no number on it, or a code box that says "not valid." Both are normal. Monday's early presale threw code errors for the first 10 to 15 minutes and then let people in.

Two things that will surprise you

You choose a section, not seats. The site assigns the row and seat, and you only see them in the cart. So the skill is picking the right kind of section fast.

Ignore the "obstructed view" label on box seats. Last year it was stuck on lots of Loge and Field Box seats that turned out fine (like this one). The truly bad pole seats in the grandstand had no label at all.

Once a device gets in

  1. Say so out loud. That device shops. The others stay where they are, but nobody starts a second checkout.
  2. Set the number of tickets first. Then look at the list of sections and prices.
  3. Go down our order (turf → field/loge box → pavilion → right-field box → grandstand) and take the first one at or under the cap. Use the list or the Best Available button. Don't click around the map.
  4. "Seats not available"? That's someone else's cart. Try again, or try the next one down. Don't leave the page.
  5. Seats in the cart. A countdown starts, about 4 to 5 minutes. Check it's the right date and the right number of tickets, then go straight to checkout. Don't go back.
  6. Sign in to MLB if it asks, let autofill drop in the card, and buy. If the bank pings the phone, approve it.
  7. Screenshot the confirmation. Then close every other device. One order per person may be all the codes allow, and we don't want two sets of tickets we can't hand to anyone.

If Saturday is gone when we get in

Check whether the other dates show up on that screen. If they don't, go back to the Ticketmaster page and use that same person's code for the next date: Emily's account has Sunday and Tuesday, Garrett's has Wednesday. Never put one person's code into the other person's account.

When it breaks (it will)

The Red Sox ticket site is famously rough. All of these happened to people on Monday or last year, and they still got tickets.

"Code not valid for this offer"

Press Submit again. And again. On Monday it started working after about 10 minutes of that. Make sure it's the code for that date and that person.

The waiting page never changes

There's no place in line to lose. On the phones, refresh by hand or reopen the link. If a laptop sits for 10 minutes, refresh that too.

"Technical difficulties" or a blank page

Switch to a phone. On Monday the phones got through when some laptops didn't.

Signed in, but nothing unlocks

Sign out and back in, or open the link in a private window. One buyer on Monday was stuck until they did.

It makes you reset your MLB password

It did that to someone mid-checkout. Just do it quickly and keep going. They still got four tickets.

"Timed out" after entering the card

Stop. Check email and "My Tickets" before trying again. Someone got charged twice. If it's unclear, call the Red Sox ticket office: 877-733-7699.

Staying out of trouble

Totally fine

  • Two real people, each with one account and their own codes
  • Four of our own devices
  • One phone on cellular
  • Refreshing by hand
  • One order, for people who are going

Don't

  • Auto-refresh extensions or scripts
  • A VPN
  • Extra accounts or email addresses
  • Codes from strangers, or giving ours away
  • Two checkouts at once on one account
  • Resale sites. Nobody has tickets yet.

If Tuesday misses

Thursday, Nov 12 at noon is round two: the general sale, plus a separate pool of seats for Mastercard World Elite cards (the JetBlue card should count). Same site, same drill.

Watch for added shows. Last year the easiest tickets were for a night that got added mid-sale.

After that: the waitlist on noahkahan.com (codes go out a few days before each show), turf seats released the afternoon of the show, and the Fenway box office that morning. People got in all three ways last July.
